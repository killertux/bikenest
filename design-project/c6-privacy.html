Privacy & data

Every request on this page is recorded in an auditable trail, and we verify your identity before acting on it. See the privacy policy for the full picture.

Export your data

A machine-readable copy (JSON) of everything we hold about you, plus a human-readable summary. Download links expire after 24 hours.

Request export

Rectification & other rights

You can also request access, rectification, restriction or objection regarding your data. Most requests are handled automatically; a few need a human pass.

  • Access — see exactly what we hold about you

  • Rectification — correct inaccurate data

  • Restriction — pause processing of your data

  • Objection — stop a specific processing purpose

Delete account

Your personal identity is removed and your sessions are signed out. Community contributions may be kept, but anonymized — they stop being attributable to you. This can’t be undone.

Consent records

Where we rely on your consent, it’s specific, recorded and withdrawable at any time — withdrawal takes effect immediately.

Delete your account?

  • Your personal identity is removed; you’re signed out everywhere and can’t sign back in.
  • Contributions you added may remain on the map, anonymized — no longer linked to you.
  • Private activity (favorites, “I parked here”, searches) is deleted.
