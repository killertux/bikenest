Behavior notes

  • Neutral confirmation (§45): unknown addresses get the identical “check your inbox” message — the response never reveals whether an account exists. Try any email, including one that clearly isn’t registered.
  • Rate-limited (§45): after the first send/resend, further attempts are throttled for 60 seconds.
  • Token lifetime: reset links expire after 1 hour (§75) and are single-use (§16).
