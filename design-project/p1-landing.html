How it works

From destination to parked bike in three steps.

  1. 01

    Set your destination

    Type an address or use your current location. BikeNest searches for parking within walking distance of where you're actually going.

  2. 02

    Compare what matters

    Cost, cover, CCTV, staffed hours and community ratings — side by side, so you can decide where you'd actually feel comfortable leaving your bike.

  3. 03

    Park — and keep it true

    After parking, confirm the spot still exists and how it looks. Every report keeps the map fresh for the next cyclist.

The community

A map kept honest by the people who use it.

BikeNest is built from contributions: cyclists add racks they know, review spots they've used, and confirm details after parking. Moderators check photos and changes before they go live — so the information you see is verified, and stays fresh.

  • Add a spot you know. New racks and stations start with one contribution — name, place, a photo.

  • Review where you parked. Ratings and notes help others pick between a busy rack and a quiet one.

  • Keep information fresh. Quick confirmations after parking mark details as verified — or flag what changed.
