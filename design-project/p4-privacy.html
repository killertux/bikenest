2. What we use data for

We process personal data only for concrete purposes tied to how BikeNest works:

  • Search & navigation. Finding parking near a destination. If you use “use my location”, the browser location is used once for that search and then discarded — it is never stored in your account or a location history.
  • Accounts & authentication. Creating and securing your account, signing in, password resets and email verification.
  • Community contributions. Publishing parking locations, photos, reviews and verification signals you choose to add, and keeping the map fresh and trustworthy.
  • Safety & moderation. Handling reports, moderating content, preventing abuse, and keeping audit records required for security and legal accountability.
  • Your privacy requests. Fulfilling access, export, correction and deletion requests you submit.

4. Legal bases

Contract

Providing the service itself: your account, saved favorites, contributions and the searches you run.

Consent

Browser geolocation — requested only after you press “use my location”, revocable at any time in your browser settings. If optional cookies are ever introduced, they will also rely on consent.

Legitimate interests

Keeping BikeNest safe: abuse prevention, moderation, security logging and incident investigation.

Legal obligation

Records we must keep by law, such as certain audit and privacy-request records.

Some providers above may process data outside Brazil. Where that happens we rely on recognized transfer mechanisms — such as standard contractual clauses — and each provider is documented with its role, the data transferred and its deletion commitments. The full provider list is reviewed before launch and kept up to date in this notice.

8. Your rights and how to use them

As a BikeNest user you can request:

  • Access to your data
  • Correction of data
  • Deletion of your account
  • Export of your data
  • Restriction or objection
  • Withdrawal of consent

How to exercise a right

  1. 1Email privacy@bikenest.example from your account address, or use the privacy section of your account settings (in the real app).
  2. 2We verify your identity before disclosing anything — your data is never released to anyone else on your behalf without verification.
  3. 3Export links, when issued, are machine-readable (JSON), expire after 24 hours, require authentication and are never publicly indexable.
  4. 4Every privacy request is logged in an audit trail so we can demonstrate it was handled.

9. Security practices

We protect personal data with measures appropriate to a community service of this size:

  • Passwords are stored only as modern hashes — never in readable form.
  • Sessions use secure, expiring cookies; deleted accounts cannot be recovered through old sessions.
  • Uploaded photos are stripped of EXIF metadata and moderated before publication.
  • Security incidents are investigated with a defined process — detection, classification, containment and personal-data impact assessment.

10. Automated decision-making

BikeNest does not make automated decisions with legal or significant effects on you. The closest things to automation are advisory helpers: freshness badges, duplicate warnings and “recommended because…” explanations. These inform your choice of where to park — they never gate your access to the service, and none of them profile you as a person.

11. Cookies

The initial version of BikeNest uses only strictly necessary, first-party cookies — principally the session cookie that keeps you signed in. No advertising or third-party tracking cookies are used. Each cookie’s purpose, lifetime and security flags are documented in the cookie policy. If analytics cookies are ever introduced, we will revisit the consent architecture and update this notice.

12. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes meaningfully, we will announce it in the app before the new version takes effect. Every version is archived below — the system records which version was shown to each user, so past notices can always be reconstructed.
