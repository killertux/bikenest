Cookie policy

BikeNest keeps cookies to a minimum: at launch it sets only the strictly necessary cookies that make sign-in and security work. No analytics or advertising cookies are used in the initial version.

1. What cookies are

Cookies are small pieces of data a website stores in your browser. BikeNest uses them for two things only: keeping you signed in securely, and protecting forms against cross-site request forgery (CSRF).

We distinguish between strictly necessary cookies — the ones the service cannot function without — and optional cookies. The table in section 2 documents every cookie the application creates: its purpose, lifetime, scope, security flags, and whether it is first- or third-party.

2. Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are set only when you sign in. You can browse the map, search and view parking locations without any cookies being set.

Sessions can be invalidated explicitly — signing out, changing your password, or reporting a security event ends the session server-side, not just in your browser.

3. Optional cookies

At launch: none. BikeNest deliberately ships without analytics, advertising or social-media cookies, so there is no cookie banner to click through and nothing optional to track you with.

If analytics or advertising cookies are introduced later, they will be listed in this table first, the privacy policy will be updated, and a consent mechanism will be added before they are set. Authentication and session cookies are part of the service's functionality and are handled securely regardless of your cookie preferences.

4. Third-party map content

The map is rendered by a third-party map provider. The provider receives the map area your browser requests — not your identity, and not your account information — as described in the privacy policy (§ Recipients).

Map tiles are loaded from the provider's servers, which may apply their own technical logging. BikeNest does not set, and does not permit the provider to set, advertising or profiling cookies through our pages.

5. Managing cookies

You can delete or block cookies in your browser settings at any time. Blocking the strictly necessary cookies has one practical effect: you will not be able to sign in, and therefore cannot add locations, verify information or manage your account. Browsing and searching the map continue to work.

Deleting your account and personal data is not a cookie setting — it is done from your account settings, as described in the privacy policy.
