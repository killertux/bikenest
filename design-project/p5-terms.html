1. The agreement

By creating an account or contributing content, you accept these terms. If you use BikeNest without an account, only the sections that apply to anonymous use (searching, viewing locations, this agreement generally) bind you.

BikeNest is also governed by our privacy policy and cookie policy, which explain how we handle personal data.

2. Accounts

An account requires an email address and a password. You are responsible for keeping your credentials confidential and for activity that happens under your account. If you suspect unauthorized access, change your password immediately and contact us — sessions can be invalidated remotely.

You must give accurate information in your account profile and in any report or verification you submit. One person may hold one primary account; creating accounts to evade restrictions or to amplify verification signals is not allowed.

You can delete your account at any time from your account settings. Deletion removes your identity from the service; your past contributions remain as anonymous community content so that parking data stays useful to other cyclists.

3. Contributions

Contributions include adding or editing parking locations, reviews, photos and verification signals such as "I parked here". You keep the rights to what you create. By contributing, you grant BikeNest a licence to store, adapt, display and distribute that content as part of the service — for example, resizing a photo or showing a review on a location page.

You confirm that you have the right to contribute what you submit, that photos you upload were taken by you and do not identify bystanders unnecessarily, and that information you add about a parking location is accurate to the best of your knowledge at the time you add it.

Contributions are reviewed before they become publicly visible, and remain subject to moderation afterwards. We may edit for clarity, hide or remove content that breaks these terms, and invalidate location data that no longer describes a real parking option.

4. Verification & freshness

Much of the information on BikeNest comes from cyclists, not from the businesses or public bodies that operate parking. We label it accordingly:

  • "Verified" means a community signal. It records that users recently confirmed a detail — a parking type, a security feature, that they parked there. It is not an inspection by BikeNest and not a statement about how safe a location is.
  • Freshness is a timestamp, not a promise. "Verified 2 days ago" tells you when information was last confirmed. Categories such as Fresh, Aging or Stale are product defaults for reading older data; they are not claims about factual validity today.
  • Check before you rely on a spot. Openings, staffing and security can change at any time. BikeNest does not guarantee that a listed location exists, is open, is free or is secure when you arrive.

Aggregated verification counts never identify individual users, and individual parking histories are never public. Details are in the privacy policy.

5. Acceptable use

You agree not to:

  • add, verify or report information you know to be false, or submit verification signals for locations you have not used;
  • use the service to harass other contributors, or upload photos or reviews whose purpose is to identify or demean individuals;
  • scrape, bulk-download or reuse the dataset in ways that violate the privacy commitments described in the privacy policy — particularly attempts to reconstruct individuals' parking histories or identities;
  • interfere with the service, circumvent rate limits or abuse-prevention measures, or access data through undocumented interfaces;
  • use automated systems to create accounts or generate contributions.

Reports submitted through the report feature must be made in good faith. Deliberately false reports waste moderator time and can hide useful parking from other cyclists.

6. Moderation & suspension

Moderators review reports and proposed changes, hide inappropriate reviews and photos, invalidate locations that no longer exist, and suspend accounts where authorized. Moderation actions are recorded in an audit trail that moderators cannot silently alter.

If your contribution is hidden or your account is suspended, we will tell you which rule was applied where practical. You can contest a decision by replying to the notification or contacting [moderation contact — to be defined]; contests are reviewed by a person, and content is restored where a decision was wrong.
