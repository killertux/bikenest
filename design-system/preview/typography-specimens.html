Rules

  • Headings are bold, tracking-tight, with balanced wrapping (text-wrap: balance).
  • Body copy sits at 15–16px with 1.6 line height; meta at 13px in muted.
  • Badges and counts run 11–12px semibold; data values in mono.
  • Sentence case everywhere — headings, buttons, labels.
