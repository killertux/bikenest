## What we collect

- **Account data** — an email address (used for sign-in, email verification and
  password reset), an optional display name, and — if you sign in with Google —
  the Google account identifier and email.
- **Community contributions** — parking locations, photos, reviews, verifications
  and proposals you submit, together with timestamps.
- **Technical data** — server-side session identifiers, security tokens and audit
  records that keep the service secure.

## How we use it

We use your data to provide the parking-finding service, to keep community
contributions accurate, and to protect the service and its users. We do **not**
sell your personal data and we do not run advertising or cross-site tracking.

## Your rights

You may request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, ask us to delete your
account (which anonymizes your contributions while keeping the dataset), or
object to or restrict specific processing. See the account privacy page.

## Cookies

We set only a small number of necessary cookies (see the Cookie Policy). We do
not use optional, advertising or analytics cookies.
